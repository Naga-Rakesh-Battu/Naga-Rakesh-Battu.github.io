SkyMind: LLM Chatbot Platform In development

Co-built with a teammate. Won the preliminary round at the UNT AI Expo (Nov 2026). My tracks: Serve and Scale.

  • A company states its needs (users, latency, budget) and adds documents. SkyMind plans capacity and cost, deploys a document-grounded chatbot and reports usage, including p95 latency.
  • RAG with BM25 and hybrid embedding search, a second-look retry for missed answers, and small/large model routing across Claude, Gemini and Ollama with budget caps. Answers stream over Server-Sent Events.
  • 93% correct on 121 held-out questions, every unanswerable question refused. The smaller model nearly matched the larger one at 1/27 of the cost ($0.16 vs $4.42 per 1,000 answers).
  • 14 of 14 common web attacks blocked in CI, Google sign-in with CSRF and SSRF protection, 17 test suites, deployed on Render.
  • Serve and Scale: load tester and capacity planner built; p95 load results in progress; Kubernetes autoscaling planned.
